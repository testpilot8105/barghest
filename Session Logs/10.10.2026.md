### Session Note: BARGHEST plumbing, photos as files (section 2.11)

Third step of this sitting, from the planner's note "photos" in the Photos 10.10 handoff, built under the defaults the owner was asked about in one message: no new package (the metadata reader is ours, no HEIC or TIFF converter). Import accepts jpg, jpeg, png, webp, gif, svg, avif, bmp, tif, tiff, heic and heif; `health.formats` lists them. On import the engine reads a photo's own metadata with no model (JPEG APP1, PNG eXIf, the WebP EXIF chunk, TIFF, the Exif item of HEIC, HEIF and AVIF; sizes from each container) into source properties on the Document: takenAt, latitude, longitude, cameraMake, cameraModel, width, height, orientation. A time with no zone is read as Philippine time (+08:00). Status is `photo`, one plain-text passage says what was read in the file's own clock, `extraction` is `photo`; `extract` refuses a photo with 400 and `extract-all` skips them. `GET file` serves images with their content type and `content-security-policy: sandbox` (an SVG's scripts never run), and every file with nosniff.

Ontology: Document gained the eight photo properties, every entity type the ontology-owned `photoId`, Relationship the `fromDocumentId` and `toDocumentId` ends with links; ontology rebuilt, sdk regenerated. Routes: `link-image` (a `depicts` Relationship from the Document to the object, method analyst, confirmed, confidence 1, observed at the date taken, the photo's passage as evidence; the first linked photo becomes the object's picture), `unlink-image` (another linked photo takes over or the picture clears), `set-photo` (links first when needed; null clears). The timeline tags a linked photo `analyst`; deleting a photo removes its links and clears the pictures it was (`picturesCleared` in the audit row). Reads: `ObjectView.photo` and `images`, `photoUrl` on rows and search results, `photoId` and `photoUrl` on `what-connects` objects, `photo` and `depicts` on the file view; `isImage` and `hasNoPreview` in the data layer; `linkImage`, `unlinkImage` and `setPhoto` wrappers.
