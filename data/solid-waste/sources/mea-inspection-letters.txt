MAHARLIKA ENVIRONMENTAL AGENCY, REGION 20
16 March 2026

HON. OFELIA S. CABUHAT
City Mayor, City of Dalumat, Province of Salimbayan

Subject: Results of the 5 March 2026 inspection of the Dalumat Sanitary Landfill, Barangay Ilaya Kanluran

Dear Madam Mayor:

This refers to the inspection conducted on 5 March 2026 by Engr. Corazon V. Lumbres, Environmental Management Specialist, at the Dalumat Sanitary Landfill, Barangay Ilaya Kanluran, City of Dalumat, in the presence of Ma. Teresa Q. Salvador, City Environment and Natural Resources Officer.

The facility was found compliant with the conditions of its Environmental Compliance Certificate, with the following findings: (a) the freeboard of the leachate collection pond was below the required level; (b) daily cover was not applied on two days in the week of inspection. During the inspection, truck NCL 8820 (body no. MHS-05) of Mabansag Hauling Services was weighed in and unloaded at the working face. The City is requested to correct the findings within thirty (30) days.

Very truly yours,
ENGR. NESTOR A. CABANILLA
Regional Director

MAHARLIKA ENVIRONMENTAL AGENCY, REGION 20
17 March 2026

HON. ANSELMO G. VERANO
Municipal Mayor, Municipality of Tumanaw, Province of Salimbayan

Subject: NOTICE OF VIOLATION, Tumanaw Open Dumpsite, Barangay Ugong Bayan

Dear Honorable Mayor:

An inspection of the Tumanaw Open Dumpsite in Barangay Ugong Bayan was conducted on 12 March 2026 by Engr. Corazon V. Lumbres, in the presence of Engr. Danilo A. Salvador, Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Officer. The inspection found open dumping, no daily cover, and burning of waste in the area. Trucks NBC 4172 (body no. MHS-03) and NBC 4175 (body no. MHS-07) of Mabansag Hauling Svcs., Inc. were seen unloading waste at the dumpsite during the inspection.

The operation of an open dumpsite is prohibited under Section 37 of the Solid Waste Act, and the matter is subject to Section 48 of the same Act. This is a Notice of Violation. You are requested to submit a written explanation within seven (7) days from receipt, after which a technical conference will be scheduled. This notice is not a finding of liability and no person is charged by it.

Very truly yours,
ENGR. NESTOR A. CABANILLA
Regional Director
