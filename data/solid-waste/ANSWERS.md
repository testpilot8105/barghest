## The hauler: three spellings, two registration numbers, one Tax ID

H-001 appears as "Mabansag Hauling Services", "Mabansag Hauling Svcs., Inc." and "Mabansag Hauling" (the SPP file prints them in capitals). It carries an state registration number (CS201906274, in `hauling-contracts.pdf`), a SPP Registration No. (201911-094217-2481733095, in `spp-awards.csv`) and one Tax ID (417-382-905-000, in both). Two of the three contract excerpts and one SPP row carry no registration number, which is what lets all spellings join into one hauler even though the two numbers differ.

- MRF reports: Mulawin Dulo and Punta Silangan have "no report" for Q3 2025. The other four barangays report all quarters.

- Audit memo: AOM No. 2026-007 (20 April 2026) names TMN-SWM-2025-009 (Php 3,720,000.00) and SEL-SWM-2025-014 (Php 4,860,000.00) as listing the same four trucks, body nos. MHS-03, MHS-05, MHS-07 and MHS-09. It asks for comment within 15 calendar days and states it is not a finding of fraud.
- Inspections: the Dalumat Sanitary Landfill (5 March 2026) is compliant with findings and notes truck NCL 8820; the Tumanaw Open Dumpsite (12 March 2026) gets a Notice of Violation that names NBC 4172 and NBC 4175 unloading. The notice states it is not a finding of liability.
- Demo question: "Which hauler serves more than one LGU and what did the inspections say" resolves to H-001, San Eladio and Tumanaw, the two inspections above.

## Design notes

- The hauler names (Mabansag, Mabansay) are invented; the proofs look for "mabansag".
- The landfill document is an Environmental Compliance Certificate (Solid Waste Act, section 38), in `mea-landfill-permit.txt`.
- The hauler carries a state registration number in one file and an SPP registration number in another, on purpose; only the Tax ID appears in both.
- The trip logs carry an `LGU` column and a `Logged At` facility (San Eladio Weighbridge, Tumanaw Transfer Station), so "same plate, two LGUs" is a vehicle logged at two facilities in two LGUs.
- The contract excerpts name the president as the hauler's representative; nobody signs anything.
