# BARGHEST: the school incident

Fourteen files shaped like the registers they imitate, for a local model to read one passage at a time. The school, city, province, agencies, units, officers, accounts, serial numbers and report numbers are all made up: the Maharlika Police Department, its Crime Lab and Cyber Crimes Unit, the Office of Schools and the National Cyber Task Force stand in for the kinds of offices that keep such registers. No real case is reconstructed.

## Files, in `sources/`

- `field-report.pdf`: MPD Field Report, memo layout, three pages. The incident, the victims, the two suspects, both firearms with serials and evidence tags, the investigator.
- `mpd-blotter.txt`: MPD Blotter Incident Record. The same people in capitals without the ñ, three Dela Peñas side by side, the statements about the missing firearms, the charges, a follow-up entry.
- `school-records.csv`: Student Records System export, five learners. Full legal names, 12-digit student IDs, sections, advisers, guardians.
- `guidance-intake-sheets.txt`: two Anti-Bullying Intake Sheets (Anti-Bullying Policy, Form B). The May complaint and its witness, with the nicknames "Wacky" and "Marc".
- `fld-firearm-records.csv`: MPD Firearms Licensing Division records. The government pistol registered to the MPD and the civilian revolver under a licence.
- `mpd-pir.txt`: Property Issuance Receipt, standard property form layout. The service pistol's serial, FLD reference and end-user.
- `firearms-identification-report.pdf`: MPD Crime Lab firearms identification report, one page, the file imported live. Sixteen evidence tags each matched to a serial, citing the ballistics database.
- `discord-export.json`: Discord server export. Accounts, channels, joins, a few messages, "wipe everything.", and the audit entry for the fourteen deleted messages.
- `messenger-thread.txt`: Messenger export from the examined handset. The move to Messenger, "add me hollow.ix", "nakuha ko na".
- `roblox-chat.txt`: Roblox chat export from the same handset. First contact in a game group, by handle.
- `tiktok-takedown.csv`: platform takedown record. Three removed videos by handle and date, category only.
- `ccu-intel-report.pdf`: MPD-CCU intelligence report, three pages. The subject's four accounts in one table, the handler with three aliases and no name, and the network claims worded as assessments.
- `nctf-advisory.txt`: NCTF advisory. The network and the ideology in general terms, as allegations.
- `livestream-metadata.json`: platform record. Stream id, start, report and removal times.

## Size and import time

25 passages in all, about 25 minutes of extraction at roughly a minute per passage. `firearms-identification-report.pdf` is one passage. PDFs are A4 in Arial with a footer line on every page; text files carry the marker line first.
