D. NARRATIVE OF THE INCIDENT
At about 0842H on 14 September 2026, per the reporting person, shots were fired inside the junior high building of Maharlika National High School. The north emergency exit had been unlocked since 0730H. Personnel of this station arrived at 0851H. DELA PENA, JOAQUIN and VILLARIN, MARCO were taken into custody at 0858H. Two firearms were recovered by the Crime Scene Unit at 0905H: a Glock 17 pistol, Serial No. BHPZ417 (tag E-001), and an Armscor M200 revolver, Serial No. RIA38-1177093 (tag E-002).

DELA PENA, JOAQUIN lives in Barangay Mabini with relatives. His father, DELA PENA, NESTOR, 44, is a seafarer working overseas and has been away from the home since February 2026. His aunt, Sgt. ROWENA DELA PENA, 38, of the Patrol Division, San Mateo Station, holds the Glock 17 pistol, Serial No. BHPZ417, under a Property Issuance Receipt. By her statement the pistol was kept in a locked plastic locker at her residence. She last saw it there at about 2140H on 13 September 2026 and found it missing at about 2215H. It is believed that DELA PENA, JOAQUIN took the pistol from the locker between those times.

By the statement of VILLARIN, RAMON, 47, father of VILLARIN, MARCO, the Armscor M200 revolver, Serial No. RIA38-1177093, licensed to him, was kept in the glovebox of the family vehicle. He found it missing on the evening of 13 September 2026. VILLARIN, MARCO is believed to have taken the revolver from the vehicle.

CHARGES DRAFTED: Murder; Frustrated Murder. Handling of the children in conflict with the law is under the Juvenile Justice Act.

FOLLOW-UP ENTRY, 17 September 2026: The Internal Affairs Division opened administrative case IAD-2026-0771 (grave misconduct, neglect of duty) against Sgt. ROWENA DELA PENA concerning the storage of the service pistol.
