Date: 21 September 2026
Subject: Online networks that target minors, and the platform letters of 21 September 2026

1. BACKGROUND. Following recent school incidents, law enforcement and researchers have described a decentralised online network known as 764. Open-source reports describe it as a loose set of private servers and channels in which minors are contacted through games and social platforms. These descriptions are allegations and assessments, not findings of any court. A related label, No Lives Matter (NLM), is described in open-source analysis as a sub-network that shares violent content. Whether any server or person in the Philippines belongs to either is for validation.

2. INDICATORS, IN GENERAL TERMS. An unfamiliar adult who contacts a minor in game chat. A request to move the conversation to another platform. Violent content on a child's device. A child who is withdrawn or who deletes messages.

3. PLATFORM LETTERS. On 21 September 2026 the CICC wrote to Discord and Reddit asking each to appoint a local representative and to respond to reports within 24 hours.

4. WHAT SCHOOLS AND PARENTS CAN DO. Report to the school's Child Protection Committee, to the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group, or call 911 if a child is in immediate danger. Keep messages and content for investigators; do not delete them.
