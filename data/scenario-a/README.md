# BARGHEST Scenario A: the school incident

Fourteen files shaped like the Philippine registers they imitate, for a local model to read one passage at a time. The school, city, province, units, officers, accounts, serial numbers and report numbers are all made up. Real agencies (PNP, DepEd, CICC) are named because the register is the point. No real case is reconstructed. The school's name is also the name of several real schools; the province, city and barangay around it are fictional.

## Files, in `sources/`

- `spot-report.pdf`: PNP Spot Report, memo layout, three pages. The incident, the victims, the two suspects, both firearms with serials and evidence tags, the investigator.
- `irf-blotter.txt`: PNP e-Blotter Incident Record Form. The same people in capitals without the ñ, three Dela Peñas side by side, the statements about the missing firearms, the charges, a follow-up entry.
- `deped-lis-export.csv`: DepEd Learner Information System export, five learners. Full legal names, 12-digit LRNs, sections, advisers, guardians.
- `guidance-intake-sheets.txt`: two Anti-Bullying Intake Sheets (DepEd Order 55 s. 2013, Annex B). The May complaint and its witness, with the nicknames "Wacky" and "Marc".
- `feo-firearm-records.csv`: PNP Firearms and Explosives Office records. The government pistol registered to the PNP and the civilian revolver under a licence.
- `pnp-par.txt`: Property Acknowledgment Receipt, COA Appendix 71 layout. The service pistol's serial, FEO reference and end-user.
- `firearms-identification-report.pdf`: PNP Forensic Group firearms identification report, one page, the file imported live. Sixteen evidence tags each matched to a serial, citing IBIS.
- `discord-export.json`: Discord server export. Accounts, channels, joins, a few messages, "wipe everything.", and the audit entry for the fourteen deleted messages.
- `messenger-thread.txt`: Messenger export from the examined handset. The move to Messenger, "add me hollow.ix", "nakuha ko na".
- `roblox-chat.txt`: Roblox chat export from the same handset. First contact in a game group, by handle.
- `tiktok-takedown.csv`: platform takedown record. Three removed videos by handle and date, category only.
- `acg-intel-report.pdf`: PNP-ACG intelligence report, three pages. The subject's four accounts in one table, the handler with three aliases and no name, and the network claims worded as assessments.
- `cicc-advisory.txt`: CICC advisory. The network and the ideology in general terms, as allegations.
- `livestream-metadata.json`: platform record. Stream id, start, report and removal times.

## Size and import time

25 passages in all, about 25 minutes of extraction at roughly a minute per passage. `firearms-identification-report.pdf` is one passage. PDFs are A4 in Arial with a footer line on every page; text files carry the marker line first.

## Answers

`ANSWERS.md` lists the planted links in plain English and `key.json` holds the same facts for a machine. Both say where this pack differs from the cast on purpose. The firearms report follows the standard crime-lab layout, because no public sample of the PNP Forensic Group's own format was found.
